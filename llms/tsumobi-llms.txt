# Tsumobi

> Tsumobi was a Y Combinator (Winter 2007) mobile messaging startup from Boston, founded by Joshua Wilson and Adam Bouhenguel and later backed by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). It built a language on top of J2ME that let feature-phone applications be downloaded and updated on the fly via URLs.
